DOrSU eSPORTS

JavaScript HTML & CSS Vercel

Problem

DOrSU eSPORTS is the official esports organization of Davao Oriental State University — home of the Stallions. The community was active, but everything ran on scattered posts: tournament announcements buried in feeds, team rosters managed in comments, and no single place where a student could find the next event or sign up their squad.

Approach

The platform was designed to give the campus esports community one dedicated hub. Every feature solves a real organizational pain point for running tournaments and teams.

  • Tournament matching — a structured home for brackets and matchups, so players always know who they're facing
  • Team registrations — squads register and manage their rosters in one place instead of comment threads
  • Announcements — official event updates that don't get lost in social media noise
  • Community hub — a permanent online home for the Stallions and the broader campus esports scene

Implementation

Built on a JavaScript front end with a clean, game-adjacent visual style and deployed on Vercel. Because most students browse on phones between classes, responsiveness was a first-class concern — tournament info and registration flows were designed mobile-first and tested on real devices.

Result

The platform is live and serves as the official home of the organization. Roster information and announcements now have one canonical source, which makes organizing tournaments noticeably less chaotic.
